MarketCompass in one paragraph

MarketCompass brings financial-market monitoring, watchlists, portfolio tracking and price alerts to Chrome and Microsoft Edge. Its Dashboard, separate Widget and toolbar Popup provide different ways to work with shared data in the same browser profile. It covers multiple asset classes with provider-dependent quotes and historical information. A Free plan and a Pro subscription offer different capacity and portfolio features.

Review access and questions

Contact marketcompassapp@gmail.com for review inquiries. Reviewer access is subject to arrangement; a public trial is not currently configured. Complimentary access or sponsorship, if provided, should be disclosed. We welcome independent assessments, not a promise of favorable coverage.
