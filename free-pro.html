When subscribing

Confirm the current price, applicable taxes, renewal and cancellation terms in the hosted checkout before purchasing. Online activation and periodic verification are required for Pro. Payments are handled by an independent billing platform, not this website.

Data and plan limits

Pro does not turn every provider into a real-time feed. Source availability, API-key requirements and historical coverage still apply.

Saved records are retained when access changes. Records outside Free allowances become read-only; retention does not mean all records continue refreshing or all alerts remain armed.

Portfolio transaction accounting uses average cost and is not a brokerage or tax-filing service.

Set up your market data sources

MarketCompass supports personal API credentials from Tiingo, Finnhub and CoinGecko. Configure all three for the fullest supported coverage. Each credential enables its corresponding feeds; you do not need all three simply to open or use the extension. Other sources work without a personal API key.

A MarketCompass Pro subscription does not include a subscription to any data provider or unlock provider-restricted data.
